Sign in gateway

Three roles. One controlled entrance.

Staff, parents, and students all enter here. Same front door, different visibility after login. That is cleaner than pretending each role needs a different public entry page.

Admin

Students, users, homestays, travel, documents, compliance, and audit-sensitive actions.

Parent

Child status, welfare feed, consent records, and emergency information.

Student

Emergency contact, handbook, check-ins, homestay details, and concern reporting.

Brutal note

Do not build self-service sign-up for a system managing children and safeguarding records.

PRD gap

Epic 1 says authentication matters but never defines invite, reset, lockout, role boundaries, or first-login mechanics. That is not a small omission. That is the operational skeleton.
