Low-level concerns register

Patterns matter before they become crises

AEGIS Standard 6.5 requires a low-level concerns policy. This is the operational surface: a register of behaviours, comments, or patterns that don't meet the threshold for formal concern but need recording and monitoring.

Brutal product note

The PRD mentions nothing about low-level concerns. KCSIE and AEGIS both require them. Low-level concerns are the early warning system: a single comment from a homestay about a student "seeming off" might mean nothing, but three comments in two months create a pattern. This register exists so patterns don't vanish into separate notes across different records.

What belongs here vs. escalated concerns

Low-level concernA comment, behaviour, or observation that doesn't meet the threshold for safeguarding action but should be recorded in case a pattern emerges.
Escalated concernA disclosure, allegation, or observation that requires immediate DSL assessment and may require external referral.
Escalation triggerIf multiple low-level concerns about the same subject emerge, or if a single concern reveals something more serious on review, escalate to DSL.
