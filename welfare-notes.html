Brutal product note

The PRD mentions "welfare tracking" exactly once. It says nothing about cadence, log structure, follow-up ownership, or how notes relate to concern escalation. What you're looking at is what the PRD should have specified: welfare notes are first-class operational records, not "comments on a student profile." Every note has an owner, a timestamp, and an explicit next action. Some notes escalate. Some resolve. All are auditable.
