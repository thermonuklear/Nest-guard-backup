If removal from school is required

AEGIS Appendix 11 requires a student removal form completed by the school before the guardian organisation takes custody of a student for mental health or welfare reasons. This form documents:

  • Reason for removal
  • School's assessment of risk
  • Handoff arrangements and responsible party
  • Return-to-school conditions
