Brutal product note

Closed concerns are not inactive data — they're the evidence trail that AEGIS inspectors check first. A closed concern with no outcome note, no closure date, and no named decision-maker is worthless as a compliance record. Every concern closed here must show who made the decision and when.

Retention and record-keeping

Do not deleteResolved concerns must be retained in full, including all notes, dates, and decision rationale. Deletion of safeguarding records is a compliance failure.
Audit trailEach record shows the original log date, closure date, source, and current status. This is the evidence AEGIS inspectors will request.
ReopeningIf new information emerges about a resolved concern, it should be re-opened as a new concern referencing the historical record — not edited in place.
