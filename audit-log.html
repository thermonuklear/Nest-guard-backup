Why audit trails matter for AEGIS

  • Standard 2.5 requires "secure and efficient" record keeping — audit trails prove records weren't backdated or altered.
  • Safeguarding records must show who accessed what — inspectors will ask.
  • DBS checks must show who saw the original certificate — this is an audit question.
  • Parent complaints may require proving what was communicated and when.
  • The PRD doesn't mention audit trails at all. This is infrastructure, not a feature — but it's essential.
