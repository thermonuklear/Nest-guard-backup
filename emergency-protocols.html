What the PRD gets wrong about emergencies

  • "24-hour contact" without specifying who, how, and what happens when they don't answer.
  • No mention of sole-guardian backup — Standard 2.6.3 requires vetted emergency cover.
  • No death/serious injury protocol — this needs to exist even if you hope it's never used.
  • Pandemic procedures are not optional post-2020 — Standard 2.6.2 explicitly includes this.
  • Response times should be tracked. "We have a number" is not the same as "we respond quickly."
