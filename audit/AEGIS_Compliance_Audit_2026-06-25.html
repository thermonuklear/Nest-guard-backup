AEGIS Compliance Audit — Oxford Guardians Platform

Re-audit · 25 June 2026 · supersedes the audit of 22 June 2026

IMPORTANT This audit re-maps every requirement of the 76-page AEGIS Accreditation Handbook & Guardianship Organisation Quality Standards (September 2025) against the platform as it stands today. It distinguishes what is genuinely functional (dynamic, reads & writes through assets/db.js, persists across sessions) from what is a static mockup (hardcoded data, no persistence) or missing entirely. It replaces the audit of 22 June 2026 and records the substantial functionality added since — most of the homestay health-&-safety suite, private-fostering casework, school- and parent-liaison logging, and pre-placement risk assessment have moved from mockup to database-backed. The data-integrity defect flagged in the previous audit has also been corrected.

PROGRESS SINCE LAST AUDIT Roughly 57% of mapped requirements are now genuinely functional (65 of 115), up from ~45% at the 22 June re-audit. Functional items rose from 52 to 65, mockups fell from 12 to 5, and missing items fell from 41 to 32. Newly database-backed since the last audit: pre-placement student risk assessments, the full homestay H&S / annual-visit / training / accident records, host self-declarations (Appendix 5), private-fostering casework (LA notification, briefings, correspondence log), school-liaison logging, and a parent communication log. The compliance-alert field mismatch reported last time has been fixed.
CAUTION Functionality is not the same as compliance. Every record still lives in browser localStorage — unencrypted, browser-bound, with no access control, audit trail or backup (fails Std 2.5.1, 2.7.4, 3.4.3, 6.2.6). The mandatory policies, handbooks, contracts and insurance/ICO evidence (the Appendix A submission set) still have no home in the platform, and the complaints standard (Std 7) remains entirely absent. An AEGIS inspector would still find the platform non-compliant in its current state — but materially closer than at the last audit.

RESOLVED — DATA-INTEGRITY DEFECT FROM PREVIOUS AUDIT The previous audit reported that generateComplianceAlerts read student consent / health / contact fields under names that did not match the saved schema, causing consent, medical-cover and UK-contact alerts to mis-fire for every student. This is now fixed: checkStudent reads legal.permissions.medical/travel/photo/data, health.privateMedicalCover and family.ukFamilyContacts — matching the stored schema. The notifications screen now reflects the actual data held per student.

Priority Gaps for AEGIS Inspection Readiness
Critical (would fail inspection)
  1. Storage & security architecture — The entire platform still runs on browser localStorage — unencrypted, single-browser, no access control, no audit trail, lost on cache-clear. Fails Std 2.5.1, 2.7.4, 3.4.3 and 6.2.6 and undermines every otherwise-functional record. Migrate to a secured, access-controlled, backed-up datastore. This is now the single largest barrier to accreditation.
  2. Policy & document management — No store for the mandatory AEGIS policies and handbooks (safeguarding, whistleblowing, Prevent, missing-students, behaviour, anti-bullying, online-safety, complaints, data-protection, safer-recruitment + student/parent/homestay handbooks) with publication/review dates — the core of the Appendix A submission set (Std 2.3, 2.7, 2.8.1, 2.10, 6.1, 6.4, 6.6, 6.7, 6.8, 6.9, 6.10, 7).
  3. Complaints system — No complaints policy, workflow or record whatsoever (Std 7). Confirmed absent — no complaints page exists in the platform.
High (expected, especially at Gold Standard)
  1. Contract management — No staff, parent, homestay or agent contracts (Std 2.2).
  2. ICO registration & insurance evidence — No tracking of ICO registration, data controller or insurance certificates (Std 2.4, 2.7.2).
  3. Safeguarding self-reporting to AEGIS — Casework is logged internally but there is no route to self-report notifiable incidents to AEGIS (Std 6.5.3).
  4. Settled-status & structured household evidence — Carer settled-status evidence and per-member household data are still flags / free-text (Std 2.1.8, 5.1.1, 5.2.1).
Medium (required, lower priority)
  1. Private-fostering 28-day auto-detection — Structured PF casework now persists, but the 28-day threshold is not computed from actual placement durations and some dashboard counts remain hardcoded (Std 8.1).
  2. Per-trip travel permissions, taxi-firm DBS confirmation & permission-to-travel letter — Std 4.2, 4.3 and Appendix 9.
  3. Written service confirmation to schools — Still a static mockup; not generated per student/year (Std 9.3).
  4. Medicine-administration record & induction programme — Std 3.4.4 and 3.1 (induction remains a static checklist).
  5. Public statement of aims & introductory-meeting records — Std 1.1, 3.1.2.
  6. Dedicated accident book — Homestay incidents persist via og_incidents, but the named accident-book register (homestay-accident.html) is still static (Std 5.10.1.2).
DIRECTION OF TRAVEL Since the first audit (~18% functional) the platform has reached ~57% functional. The remaining work is now concentrated in two areas rather than spread across every standard: (1) the underlying secure datastore, and (2) a policy / document-management module covering the Appendix A submission set and complaints. Closing those two would move the majority of the remaining 32 missing items toward compliance.
