Development Plan

Build sequence to AEGIS readiness — Oxford Guardians Platform

Prepared 7 August 2026  ·  Basis AEGIS Architecture Review, 7 Aug (59% weighted)
Constraints database security and certification sequenced last; policy content slotted in once every place that holds it exists.

How this is ordered

By dependency — what must be true before the next thing can be built or verified. Not by compliance gain.

Instruments before workThe dashboard and alert engine are how every later stage is verified. While they show hardcoded fiction, you cannot tell whether anything you build works.
Plumbing before featuresThe alert engine and audit interceptor are single choke points in db.js. Every store added after inherits them free.
Containers before contentPolicies slot into a store plus a category, a manifest, an acknowledgement record and a public surface. Stage 3 builds all of it.
Facts before enforcementA rule can only be enforced on a field that exists. Enforcement follows schema, never precedes it.

Three calls to overrule if you disagree

1 · Audit interceptor at Stage 1, not Stage 9. It is Standard 2.5.1 record-keeping rather than access control, and it is one choke point in db.js — added now, every store in Stages 2–7 inherits it free. Added at Stage 9, it is a pass back over six new stores. The access-control layer over it stays at Stage 9. Costs ~1 day of rework if you'd rather hold the line strictly.
2 · old_version/ → root promotion at Stage 0. It only gets more expensive as the diff grows, and the directory name is itself how a mirror came to be mistaken for the live app.
3 · Stage 5 before Stage 6. You cannot enforce a rule on a field that does not exist yet — homestay-create.html currently calls savePlacement() with zero validation, so all of enforcement is genuinely new work.

Stages 0–3 (~2.5 weeks) deliver the largest single jump, because Stage 3 ends with your existing policy content landing against complete infrastructure. Stages 0–8 bring the platform to functional AEGIS coverage; Stages 9–10 make it defensible in an actual inspection.
