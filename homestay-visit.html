What the annual visit must cover (5.10.2, 5.10.3)

Smoke alarmsTest all alarms. Confirm presence on each floor.
CO alarmTest if gas appliances present. Confirm location near boiler.
Gas safety certificateCheck expiry date. Must be valid.
Electrical safetyVisual check. No frayed cables, overloaded sockets, or unsafe appliances.
Evacuation routeDiscuss and confirm clear route from student bedroom to exit.
First aid basicsConfirm first aid kit present and carers know location.
Medication storageSecure storage for any student medications. Alcohol secured from minors.
Food hygieneKitchen cleanliness. Allergen awareness discussed.
Household changesConfirm no new residents since last visit. Any new DBS needed?
