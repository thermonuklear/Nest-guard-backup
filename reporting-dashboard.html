What inspectors actually look for

  • Evidence, not policies. Anyone can write a policy — show me the DBS certificates, the training records, the welfare notes.
  • Dated documents. If it doesn't have a date, it could have been created yesterday.
  • Who did what. Audit trails matter. "We do this" is not the same as "Jay Ray did this on 15 April."
  • Gaps explained. If something is incomplete, say why and when it will be fixed. Silence looks like hiding.
  • Consistency. If your policy says "weekly welfare checks," your welfare log should show weekly entries.
