Why SCRs fail inspection

  • Certificate "seen" but no record of who saw it or when.
  • References "obtained" but not verified — no phone call or written confirmation.
  • Overseas checks forgotten for drivers who lived abroad.
  • Barred list check conflated with DBS — they're separate requirements for regulated activity.
  • Update service not being used — 3-year renewals become compliance debt.
  • Hire & reward insurance not on file alongside the DBS certificate.
