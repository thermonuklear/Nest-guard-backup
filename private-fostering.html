Private fostering trigger rules (Standard 8)

Age thresholdUnder 16, or under 18 if disabled
Duration threshold28+ consecutive days living with non-parent/non-close-relative
Close relative exclusionsGrandparent, sibling, aunt, uncle, step-parent (with legal responsibility)
Notification deadlineLA must be notified as soon as arrangement is known — ideally 6+ weeks before start
School awarenessOrganisation must inform school so they can also notify LA if required

Required evidence per arrangement

LA notification letterDated copy of notification sent to local authority children's services

School notificationEvidence that school has been informed of the arrangement
Parent consentWritten permission from parent for the private fostering arrangement
Homestay awarenessEvidence homestay understands their obligations under private fostering regulations

Why private fostering is often missed

  • Guardianship organisations assume schools handle notification — they often don't.
  • Rolling short stays never hit 28 days individually but arrangements functionally become private fostering.
  • 16-17 year olds are forgotten because age threshold is 16 — but disability provision extends to 18.
  • Homestays don't understand they're private fostering — they think they're just "hosting."
  • PRD doesn't mention private fostering at all. This is a legal requirement, not a compliance nice-to-have.
