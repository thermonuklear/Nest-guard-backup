Brutal product note

This is the surface the PRD never specified: what does a safeguarding case actually look like inside the system? It's not a single note—it's a timeline of actions, communications, and decisions. Every contact with the student, school, or external agency is logged. Every decision is recorded with rationale. AEGIS Standard 6.2.5 requires secure, restricted case notes. This is where they live.
