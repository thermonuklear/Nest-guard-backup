AEGIS Standard 5.10.1.2 & RIDDOR Governance Requirements

Universal Role Coverage: All accidents, minor injuries, illnesses, or near-misses involving students, homestay families, operational staff, or transport drivers must be recorded immediately.
First Aid & Medication: Ensure the certified first aider's name and any medication administered (with dosage and time) are documented.
Pattern Detection: Recurring minor incidents at a single homestay address or travel route trigger immediate risk assessment reviews.
Statutory RIDDOR & DSL Escalations: Incidents involving hospitalization >24h, fractures, or safeguarding disclosures require immediate DSL notification and statutory reporting.
