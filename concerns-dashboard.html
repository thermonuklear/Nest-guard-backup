Low-Level Concerns

Early warning system for monitoring patterns. A register of behaviours, comments, or observations that don't meet the threshold for formal concern but need recording in case a pattern emerges. AEGIS Standard 6.5 requires this register to be maintained and reviewed regularly.

View Low-Level Concerns

Escalated Concerns

Active concerns that have crossed the threshold for formal DSL assessment. Includes disclosures, allegations, and patterns that require immediate review, potential external referral, or formal written record in line with AEGIS Standard 6.5 and KCSIE.

View Escalated Concerns

Resolved (History)

Closed concerns with full audit trail. Each resolved concern retains its original log, review notes, and closure date. Historical records are maintained in line with AEGIS safeguarding record-keeping requirements and must not be deleted.
