Standards 9.2 and 9.6

  • All welfare concerns and incidents must be communicated to the student's school promptly.
  • The communication must be logged, timestamped, and attributed to a named staff member.
  • Generating this notification automatically creates a record in the School Liaison log.
  • Keep a copy of the sent message and any school response as part of the student record.
